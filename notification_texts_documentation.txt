## 10. NOTIFICATION TRIGGERS
===========================

### BOOKING LIFECYCLE
1. **Booking Created** → Customer & Salon notifications
2. **Booking Confirmed** → Customer & Salon notifications
3. **Booking Cancelled** → Customer & Salon & Employee notifications
4. **Booking Rescheduled** → Customer & Salon & Employee notifications
5. **Booking Completed** → Customer & Salon notifications
6. **No-Show** → Customer & Salon notifications

### PAYMENT LIFECYCLE
1. **Payment Received** → Salon notification
2. **Tip Given** → Customer notification
3. **Review Requested** → Customer notification

### FEEDBACK LIFECYCLE
1. **Review Submitted** → Salon & Employee notifications
